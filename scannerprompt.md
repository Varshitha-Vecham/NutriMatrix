**1. Header**

* Page title: "Product Scanner"
* Subtitle: "Scan a product barcode to quickly identify and analyze the product."

**2. Scanner Section**
Create a large centered scanner area with:

* Camera/scanner preview area
* Barcode scanning frame
* "Start Scanner" button
* "Stop Scanner" button

* Product image
* Product name
* Brand
* Barcode number
* Category

"View Nutrition Analysis"

**4. Recent Scans**
Show recently scanned products in simple cards containing:

* Product name
* Brand

**5. Empty State**

"No products scanned yet. Scan your first product to get started."

Make the page responsive for desktop and mobile.
